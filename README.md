# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

A tiny Node program that writes exactly one sentence — under 200 characters —
explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of
the year 1800, using only words from before their time.

The sentence it writes to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Picture a loom that has woven every letter, book, and song ever made into one cloth, so any man may read it, answer it, or add his own thread, all from his own chair.

The loom is the image: weaving was central to life in 1800, and the internet is
the whole of human writing woven into one cloth, readable — and answerable —
from your own chair.
